All states

Top: size of the static shift (scales as T⁴). Bottom: the dynamic shift at 300 K, which tends to the Farley-Wing limit at high n. Diamonds mark states where the static formula does not apply: the coupling to a near-degenerate partner exceeds its gap (second-order theory fails), or most of α(0) comes from partners below kBT, which the thermal field does not see as static. Use the dynamic shift for those; hover or see the table for the reason. Files made before this check have no flag and show a gap-below-10⁻⁶ a.u. warning instead.
